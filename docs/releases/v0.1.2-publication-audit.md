# Millennium Eye 0.1.2 beta verification

Date: October 21, 2026.

The attack/target/hand-reveal repair is published as 0.1.2 after final native
review. The 0.1.1 candidate was returned to draft before manager publication.
Its hidden-movement attribution was removed: a later public reveal never
identifies an earlier hidden draw or source zone through a private card ID.

## Evidence

- Release build against Master Duel 2.8.0 / Steam build 24462996 bindings:
  zero warnings/errors. The supported native hash remains unchanged.
- 28 event regressions exercise production native adapters, activation reader,
  public-record deduplication and speech policy with native/speech doubles.
- The original 21 reader checks and 25 installation checks pass.
- The same 77 Harmony targets resolve against the installed bindings.
- Independent native review found no remaining material issue after removal
  of hidden-movement attribution. Later additions to the harness cover visible
  left-only draw records and ownership of a public graveyard source.

Public movement names come only from positive native BuffMoveData card IDs.
The mod makes no raw engine identity lookup for hand additions. Multi-card
records are captured individually before grouping. Later positive hand reveals
say "Opponent reveals NAME in hand"; they do not identify an earlier hidden
move. The reader does not announce uncommitted negative cost annotations or
whole-hand UID-zero arrays.

0.1.1 loader check detected NVDA and registered 20 handlers without failed
patch entries; it does not verify live event speech or the corrected build.
